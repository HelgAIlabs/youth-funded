Our Story

A Vision for Financial Empowerment

Established in 2025, YouthFunded started with a shared vision to drive financial empowerment and literacy. Today, it is a global network of student innovators, mentors, and partners dedicated to advocating literacy, inclusion, and education for all.

Our journey has been fueled by the very people we aim to support. Every campaign, every workshop, every connection made reinforces our belief in the collective power of youth.

Our Mission

Advocating literacy, inclusion & education by specifically targeting SDG 4 (Quality Education) and SDG 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth), fostering a culture of financial empowerment and tangible social impact.

visibility

Our Vision

A world where every individual has the financial literacy and tools necessary to achieve Decent Work and Economic Growth (SDG 8), supported by accessible Quality Education (SDG 4).
